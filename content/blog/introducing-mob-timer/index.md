In the world of software development, collaboration is key — but even the best teams can get stuck on small details like *"Who drives next?"*

That’s where [**Mob Timer**](https://mob-timer.vercel.app) comes in: a lightweight, delightful tool that makes role rotation **fair, fun, and frictionless**, so your team can focus on what really matters — writing great code together.

## Why Mob Timer?

We’ve all been there:

> “Didn’t you just go?”  
> “Wait, whose turn is it?”  
> “Is it time yet?”

These micro-delays add up. They break flow, introduce bias, and can make pairing feel more like a chore than a joy.

Mob Timer solves this by combining **smart automation** with **delightful UX**:

- 🔁 Randomly assigns drivers (no repeats!)
- ⏱️ Auto-rotates using 5/10/15/20-minute presets
- 🎉 Confetti bursts when time’s up
- 👀 Shows who’s driving and who’s next — at a glance
- 💾 Saves your roster locally — no accounts, no backend
- 🔐 Runs entirely in-browser — no tracking, ever

It’s not just a timer. It’s a **collaboration rhythm engine**.

## How It Works: 3 Simple Steps

1. **Select Participants**  
   Tick who’s joining today from your saved roster.
2. **Pick a Preset**  
   Choose 5, 10, 15, or 20 minutes — or adjust anytime.
3. **Click Start**  
   We randomize the order, start the clock, and auto-rotate.

No configuration. No learning curve. Just open, click, and code together.
